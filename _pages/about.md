:relaxed: About Me
------

I am currently a Specially Appointed Associate Professor at the Innovation School of Artificial Intelligence, Hefei University of Technology. Prior to this, I received my Ph.D. (through a combined Master's and Ph.D. program) from the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Central South University, under the supervision of Prof. [Jianxin Wang](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/wangjianxin1/zh_CN/index.htm) and Assoc. Prof. [Ping Zhong](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/zhongping/zh_CN/index.htm). 

<br>My research primarily focuses on Embodied AI, Robotics, Multi-modal Perception and Fusion, and Path Planning and Decision Making. I have published over 30 academic papers—including more than 10 as the first or corresponding author—in top-tier journals and conferences such as IEEE TNNLS, IEEE TCSVT, ACM TITS, IEEE RAL, ACM MM, AAAI, IJCAI, ICRA, and IROS. Furthermore, I have led or participated in more than 10 scientific research projects. I also serve as an active reviewer for prestigious journals including IEEE TRO, IEEE TCSVT, IEEE TIM, and IEEE RAL, as well as top international conferences such as NeurIPS, CVPR, AAAI, ACM MM, ICRA, and IROS.

我现在是合肥工业大学人工智能创新学院的特任副教授。在此之前，我硕博连读毕业于中南大学计算机学院，师从[王建新](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/wangjianxin1/zh_CN/index.htm)教授和[钟萍](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/zhongping/zh_CN/index.htm)副教授。我一直从事具身智能、机器人、多模态感知与融合、路径规划与决策领域的科学研究，在IEEE TNNLS、IEEE TCSVT、ACM TITS、IEEE RAL、ACM MM、AAAI、IJCAI、ICRA、IROS等期刊和会议上累计发表学术论文30余篇（其中第一/通讯作者论文10余篇），授权发明专利4项。我主持/参与了10余项科研项目，担任IEEE TRO、IEEE TCSVT、IEEE TIM、IEEE RAL等高水平期刊和NeurIPS、CVPR、AAAI、ACM MM、ICRA、IROS等国际会议审稿人。
